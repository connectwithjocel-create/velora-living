Living Rooms

Anchor the room with a sofa scaled to the wall, then layer chairs and low tables within easy reach.

  • Float the sofa off the wall in larger rooms to create a conversation zone.
  • Choose a rug large enough for at least the front legs of every seat.
  • Mix one curved piece into a room of straight lines.

Bedrooms

Quiet palettes, a substantial headboard and warm bedside light make a bedroom feel settled.

  • Allow 24–30 inches of clearance on each side of the bed.
  • Use matching lamps for symmetry or mismatched heights for ease.
  • Keep the wall opposite the bed calm — it's the first thing you see.

Dining Rooms

Leave at least 36 inches between table edge and wall so chairs pull out comfortably.

  • Allow about 24 inches of table width per diner.
  • Hang pendants 30–36 inches above the tabletop.
  • A sideboard nearby keeps serving pieces within reach.

Small Spaces

Choose raised legs, glass and multi-use pieces to keep sightlines open.

  • Choose furniture on legs to show more floor.
  • Use wall-mounted storage to free floor area.
  • Pick pieces that serve two purposes.

Modern Minimalism

Fewer pieces, better proportions — every object earns its place.

  • Limit the palette to three colors.
  • Give each object space to be seen.
  • Hide everyday clutter in closed storage.

Warm Contemporary

Timber, texture and low sunlight bring warmth to clean modern lines.

  • Layer two wood tones at most.
  • Use 2700K bulbs in the evening.
  • Add texture with wool, linen and velvet.
