Interior Styling Services

Considered guidance for rooms that feel complete.

Introduction

Furniture Is Only Half the Room

Scale, light, color and the space between pieces decide whether a room feels finished. Velora’s styling services help you make those decisions with confidence.

Services

How We Can Help

Room Refresh

Rework an existing room using what you own plus a few targeted additions.

Furniture & Décor Selection

A curated shortlist matched to your space, palette and budget.

Space Planning

Measured layouts covering circulation, zones and furniture scale.

Color & Material Guidance

Paint, fabric and finish palettes that tie rooms together.

Design Philosophy

Calm, Personal, Lasting

We start with how a room is used, not how it photographs. Comfortable seating, light at the right heights and enough open floor come first; character follows through texture, color and a few pieces with real presence.

Rooms should evolve. We favor flexible layouts and quality basics that can take new accents over time rather than a look that dates.

The Process

Four Steps to a Finished Room

  1. Discover Your Style

    Share photos, inspiration and how you use the room. We identify the palette and mood that suit you.

  2. Define the Space

    Measurements, light and circulation become a scaled plan with clear zones.

  3. Curate the Pieces

    A shortlist of furniture, lighting and objects, with alternatives at different price points.

  4. Bring the Look Together

    Placement guidance and styling notes so the finished room feels intentional.
